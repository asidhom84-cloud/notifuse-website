Powerful Alert Rules

NotiSignal is more than following topics. You create customizable alerts that define when you get notified — thresholds, percentage moves, schedules, and event triggers — so notifications fire on your terms.

Above a value

Notify when a price or metric rises above the target you set.

Below a value

Notify when a price or metric falls below the target you set.

Percentage increase

Catch upward moves of a size you choose, not every tiny fluctuation.

Percentage decrease

Get alerted when a value drops by the percentage that matters to you.

Any change

Stay close to the market — get notified when the value moves at all.

Breaking events

Surface urgent headlines and important moments as they happen.

Scheduled alerts

Receive updates on a fixed interval — every few minutes, hours, or days.

Continuous monitoring

Your active rules keep watching in the background so you don’t have to refresh.
