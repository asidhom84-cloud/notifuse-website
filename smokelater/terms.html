Purpose of SmokeLater

SmokeLater is a tracking, delay, and reduction tool for adults who want to reduce smoking or nicotine consumption gradually. Its concept is: Delay. Reduce. Save.

The App lets you log smoking events, increase delay intervals over time, review history and delay progress, estimate avoided smoking and money saved, set a savings goal, use a craving/delay challenge, play "Beat the Wait", use "Distract Me", and receive local reminders.
