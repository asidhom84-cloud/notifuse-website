NotiBus

Bus tracking and trip updates for schools and teams

NotiBus helps schools, companies, universities and transportation teams run bus trips. Drivers and supervisors manage each trip, and authorized parents and riders can follow the bus, see estimated arrival times, and receive trip updates.

Features

Everything a trip needs, in one app

From the first stop to the last drop-off, NotiBus keeps drivers, supervisors and families on the same page.

Latest bus location

During an active trip, authorized parents, guardians and riders can see the bus on a map, along with how recently its location was updated.

ETAs and trip progress

See the next stop, trip status and estimated arrival times. Arrival times are estimates and can change with traffic and conditions.

Attendance and boarding

Drivers and supervisors record who has boarded, who is absent, and can correct entries, so everyone has a clear record of each trip.

Trip notifications

Receive updates such as a trip starting, the bus approaching a stop, boarding, and delays, according to your notification settings.

Parent and rider visibility

Parents and guardians follow the trips of the riders linked to them. Access is limited to the riders and trips you are authorized to see.

Driver and supervisor tools

Drivers start and run trips, share bus location while a trip is active, follow stops, and manage boarding. Supervisors and administrators coordinate routes, riders and trips.

Who it is for

Built for shared transportation

Schools

Coordinate school bus routes and keep families informed about each trip.

Companies

Run employee shuttles and keep riders and coordinators up to date.

Universities

Manage campus and student transport with clear boarding records.

Transportation teams

Give drivers, supervisors and administrators one place to run trips.

Good to know

Location, accuracy and privacy

Bus location is shared by the driver’s phone only while a trip is being tracked, and only with people authorized for that bus or trip. Location, speed and arrival times depend on the driver’s device permissions, GPS signal and network connection, so they can be delayed or unavailable at times. ETAs are estimates. NotiBus is an operations tool and does not replace adult supervision or your organization’s safety procedures. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
